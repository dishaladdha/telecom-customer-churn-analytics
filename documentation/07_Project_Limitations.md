The dataset represents a fictional telecommunications company.

The source contains a static customer snapshot and not historical monthly transactions.

The dashboard identifies associations and patterns but does not establish causation.

Revenue at Risk represents monthly charges associated with churned customers and is not a forecast of realized financial loss.

High Risk Customers is a descriptive label and not a predictive score.

The dashboard connects to a local MySQL database.

A production cloud-refresh configuration has not been implemented.

Retention recommendations should be tested before being applied in a real business environment.
