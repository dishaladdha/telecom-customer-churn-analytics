# Business Insights

## Insight 1

Month-to month customers have the highest churn rate (42.71%).

## Insight 2

One-year contract customers have significantly lower churn (11.27%).

## Insight 3

Two-year contract customers have the lowest churn rate (2.83%).

## Business Recommendation

Encourage month-to-month customers to move to longer-term contracts through discounts or loyalty program.


Insight 1: Contract type

Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at 42.71 percent.

One-year customers have a churn rate of 11.27 percent.

Two-year customers have the lowest churn rate at 2.83 percent.

Recommendation:

Consider targeted programmes that encourage eligible month-to-month customers to move to longer contracts.

Insight 2: Internet service

Fiber optic customers account for the highest churn volume in this dataset.

Recommendation:

Investigate service experience, support requirements, pricing, and customer expectations for Fiber optic customers.

Insight 3: Payment method

Electronic check customers account for the highest churn volume.

Recommendation:

Investigate whether payment friction, customer profile, contract mix, or communication preferences are associated with this pattern.

Insight 4: Tenure

Customers within their first 12 months represent the highest churn volume.

Recommendation:

Strengthen customer onboarding, early-life support, and retention outreach.

Insight 5: Revenue

Churned customers represent a material portion of the monthly charge base.

Recommendation:

Prioritize retention interventions using both churn volume and monthly revenue exposure.
